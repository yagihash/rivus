# Notes on bench/heavy-ledger.tsv

The ledger is written only by the tools (`make bench-passline`, `make bench-deps`; `NO_LEDGER=1` adds no row). Read it with
`python3 bench/heavy_ledger.py [--kind passline|deps|once] [--target memos] [--last N]`; the margin is computed there, not stored.
How to measure and what the lines mean: [passline-baseline.md](passline-baseline.md). The light benches are in [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md).

- **The definition of the memory changed on 2026-11-26** (decided by the user): from the sum of the RSS of the process tree to the macOS `footprint` of the tree, because the RSS of macOS keeps the pages that Go gave back (`MADV_FREE_REUSABLE`) and the RSS peak of C moved by about 0.5 GiB between equal runs while its footprint did not. Two columns were added at the END of the header (no cell of an old row moved; an old row has fewer cells, which reads as empty): `peak_footprint_gb` (GiB; the maximum same-time sum of the footprint of the tree; empty when the basis is RSS) and `mem_basis` (`footprint` or `rss`). `peak_tree_gb` keeps its meaning (the sum of the RSS) and stays as a reference. The verdict of a row is `peak_footprint_gb` when `mem_basis` is `footprint`, else `peak_tree_gb` (`verdict_gb` in `bench/heavy_ledger.py`). **rustypaste became a row of the pass line on 2026-11-26** (`decided: true`, 150 s, 8 GiB; the first three rows of that kind, `result` PASS, are the rows of that date at `b13d5134`; the older rustypaste rows are `INFO` and are not rewritten). **Rows before this date are RSS and are not rewritten** (their `mem_basis` is empty); Linux rows are RSS (`mem_basis=rss`). The lines and the three states are the same. Compare a row with the rows of the same basis.
- The pass line is four lines: A (`memos-4gib`), C (`gitea`), D (`dart_services`: converge, 8 GB, 600 s, `detected=n/a`) and cocoon. The result of a line is its newest `--kind passline` row (`python3 bench/heavy_ledger.py --target <name> --last 1`). Numbers are not copied here. The memos 8 GB line (B) was removed from the pass line on 2026-11-25; its old rows stay.
- Pass-line rows of the new lines A (`memos-4gib`) and C (`gitea`) are written with `--kind passline` like the others (decided 2026-11-24, see passline-baseline.md). The row of C records the count of detection `n/3` only, never a finding count, a list, a path or a place. The two `once` rows of Gitea below were stopped by the watchdog before this decision.
- 2026-11-23, fe7740d6: memos passed 3 times (48 to 50 s, 5.99 to 6.15 GB, converged, detected, `quiet_after_fix=no` recorded only). These runs were made before the ledger existed; the output is not kept.
- The 3 rows of 2026-11-23 at `6bee840` (go-s3, gcp, dart-gcp) were moved from `bench/deps-ledger.tsv` (removed). That file had columns that the ledger does not have; they are kept here: max_rss_mb (end-of-run peak of the main process, not the tree sum, so `peak_tree_gb` is empty) 12449, 4012, 1530; functions 5845, 20810, 4593; uncertainties 47, 143, 738. `result`: timeout, finished, finished.
- The 3 rows of 2026-11-23 at `dac8bcb6` (go-s3, gcp, dart-gcp) and the 3 old rows at `6bee840` differ in both the way of measuring (the tree sum, not the main process) and the rivus commit: do not compare them.
- The column `live_heap_max_bytes` is a guide on the upper side: the largest amount that the GC marked live (`/gc/heap/live:bytes`). It counts what was allocated while the GC was marking, so in a phase that allocates fast (the summary phase) it is larger than what is really live. It is not the real live size. `live_margin_gb` in the table (limit minus this value) is therefore on the low side. Correction of 2026-11-24 (memos, plain binary, one run: load phase 4.34 GB against 4.27 GB really live; summary phase 4.76 GB against 2.21 GB really live); see ADR-121.
- `kind` = `once` (one-off check): rows that no tool makes, written with `append_row` of `bench/heavy_ledger.py` on 2026-11-24. The numbers that have no column (functions, own code / dependencies, packages, `summary_elems`, `summary_steps`, the load time of a count-only row, the process peak) are in the `result` text. The columns `findings` and the per-category counts are left empty (the findings of a public project are not recorded). A row of `settings` "関数の数だけ。解析なし" is a count of the functions of the loaded program, without an analysis. `rivus_commit` is empty where it was not recorded. The 2 Gitea rows (`v1.26.4`, `95ba37d9`) were stopped by the 8 GB watchdog (exit -9); the load-only row shows that the limit was crossed inside the load phase (no function count). The row "内訳の測定(強制 GC のあと)" is the live heap after a forced GC in the summary phase of memos (start 1.58 GB, end of the 2nd round 2.26 GB), not a limit check.
