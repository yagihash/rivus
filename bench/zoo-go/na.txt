The cells of the Dart zoo that the Go zoo has no cell of, and that Go can have: the Go side of the parity marks (the cells that Dart has and the form does not exist in Go are @na(go: reason) in the Dart cell).
Read by TestZooDart (the parity line "zoo-go: parity marks"; zooGoMarkErrors). Not locked (bench/zoo/marks.d reads only the cells), and a PARITY @todo needs no approval (docs/DESIGN-zoo.md, 4.2.5).
A mark @todo(F/<family>/<shape>: PARITY: reason) is a form that Go can have and the cell is not written yet. The reason has no ")" (the pattern is [^)]+): "Go で未測定: <package>".
A Dart cell with no mark here is "not classified" (the second line of the test log).
